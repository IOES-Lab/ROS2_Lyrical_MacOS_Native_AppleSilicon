Observed route in the candidate source:

- OceanCurrentWorldPlugin parses the tidal database or harmonic constituents.
- OceanCurrentPlugin.cc:663-699 copies M2/S2/N2, directions and start time into
  dave_interfaces/msg/StratifiedCurrentDatabase and publishes the message.
- OceanCurrentModelPlugin.cc:199 subscribes to that database.
- OceanCurrentModelPlugin.cc:376-378 enables tide per model from tide_oscillation.
- OceanCurrentModelPlugin.cc:387 UpdateDatabase stores the metadata.
- OceanCurrentModelPlugin.cc:582-584 calls TidalOscillation::Initiate and ::Update.

Therefore the global /ocean_current payload is not the tidal-output oracle. The
documented tidal behavior is model-specific and appears on
/model/<namespace>/ocean_current.
